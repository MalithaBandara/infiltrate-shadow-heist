# Post copy for each short

Post on TikTok first, then Reels and Shorts a day or two later (same file, no watermark). Add a
trending sound in the app before posting, even at near-zero volume (YouTube: Audio Library). Pin
the comment straight after posting and reply to early comments. No em dashes.

Bio on every account: `Solo-made stealth game. Free on Google Play and the App Store.` plus the
link to the landing page (tag it per platform, e.g. `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social`, so
Play Console and the site show which platform sends installs).
